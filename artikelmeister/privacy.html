Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 30, 2026

ArtikelMeister is a free, open-source app for Android and macOS that helps you learn German noun articles (der, die, das). There are no accounts on our side, no servers of our own, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. This page explains what the app does with your data and with Google user data.

Data stored on your device

Your flashcards, study progress, streaks and settings are stored locally, in the app's private storage on your device. The app works offline. You can delete this data at any time.

Google Sign-In and permissions

Signing in with Google is optional. If you choose to sign in, the app asks for these permissions:

  • openid, userinfo.email, userinfo.profile: to show which Google account is connected.
  • drive.file: to create and update the app's own backup file in your Google Drive. With this permission the app can only access files it created itself, not your other Drive files.
  • generative-language.retriever and cloud-platform: only to send requests to Google's Gemini API for the AI word generator.

Google Drive backup

When you are signed in, the app keeps a backup of your word list, study progress and settings in a folder named ArtikelMeister Cloud Sync in your own Google Drive. It is updated automatically when you change something, and it is used to keep your devices in sync. The backup stays in your Drive. We have no access to it.

AI word generator

When you use the AI word generator, the app sends a request directly from your device to Google's Gemini API. The request contains the level you chose, the number of words, an optional topic you type, and a list of words you already have (so the AI avoids duplicates). Instead of signing in, you can use your own Gemini API key.

Credentials

Your sign-in tokens and API key are stored on your device using the platform's storage, such as the macOS Keychain. They are never sent anywhere except to Google, to authenticate you.

Google API Services User Data Policy

ArtikelMeister's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Google user data is used only to provide the features described on this page.
  • It is not sold and not used for advertising.
  • It is not used to train AI models.
  • It is not transferred to third parties.
  • Humans do not read it, unless you give permission, it is needed for security, or it is required by law.

Data sharing

We do not share your data with anyone. The only communication the app has is directly between your device and Google.

Retention, revocation and deletion

  • Your data stays until you delete it.
  • The "Delete account data" option (Hesap Verilerini Sil) in the app's Settings removes the Drive backup and the data on your device.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and you can delete the "ArtikelMeister Cloud Sync" folder from your Drive yourself.
  • On Android, uninstalling the app removes its local data. On macOS, you can remove it by deleting the folder ~/Library/Application Support/ArtikelMeister.

Security

The app connects to Google only over HTTPS. Your data stays on your device and in your own Google Drive.

Children

ArtikelMeister is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect any personal data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
