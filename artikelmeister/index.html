Tired of guessing der, die, das? So was I, so I made an app for it.

ArtikelMeister is a free flashcard app for German noun articles. It shows you every word with its article and brings it back right before you'd forget it, until die Lampe just sounds right. For Android and macOS, made for Turkish speakers.

A1 Beginner

der Tisch

masa (table)

Spoon, fork, knife. Three genders.

German gives every noun a gender, and the gender rarely has anything to do with the thing.

The girl is neuter: das Mädchen. The sun is feminine and the moon is masculine: die Sonne, der Mond. There are patterns, and every pattern has a queue of exceptions behind it.

So you guess. In the exam, in the email to your landlord, at the bakery counter. The fix is boring and it works: see each noun with its article, again and again, at the right moments.

  • der Löffelspoon, kaşık
  • die Gabelfork, çatal
  • das Messerknife, bıçak

What it does

  • Smart repetition

    Words you know fade into the background. Words you keep missing come back until they stick.

  • AI word generator

    Ask Gemini for new nouns at your level, from A1 to C1, without repeats.

  • Works offline

    Study on a train with no signal. Your cards live on your device.

Spaced repetition, minus the jargon

Your memory forgets on a schedule. ArtikelMeister reviews on a schedule too, just a little ahead of it.

The scheduler is inspired by SM-2, the classic algorithm that Anki also grew out of. You don't need to know any of that to use it.

  1. You see a noun without its article

    ___ Tisch? Say it in your head, then flip the card.

  2. You rate how it went

    Four buttons, in Turkish like the rest of the app:

    • Tekrar (again)
    • Zor (hard)
    • İyi (good)
    • Kolay (easy)

  3. The app picks the next date

    Missed it? It's back in 30 seconds. Knew it? It waits longer every time.

  4. You only study what's due

    Each day you get the cards that are due, plus new ones when you ask for them. Show up daily and your streak grows.

One new card, answered “İyi” (good) every time

The wait before each next review. Every gap is roughly two and a half times the last.

  1. 5 minafter #1
  2. 1 dayafter #2
  3. 3 daysafter #3
  4. 8 daysafter #4
  5. 20 daysafter #5
  6. 50 daysafter #6

Six good answers in, der Tisch leaves you alone for almost two months.

Out of words? Ask Gemini for more.

Pick a level from A1 to C1 and how many words you want. ArtikelMeister asks Google's Gemini for nouns with their article and Turkish meaning, and adds them to your deck.

It sends along the words you already have, so you don't get Tisch for the fifth time. You can also give it a topic, like food or work.

Sign in with your Google account or paste your own Gemini API key. The generator needs internet. Studying doesn't.

Works with zero bars of signal

Your words, decks and progress live on your device. Studying needs no internet and no account.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking. Not in the app, and not on this website either. The details are in the privacy policy.

Lives on your device

  • Your words and decks, including the ones you import
  • Your progress: review dates, ratings, streak
  • Your settings

Goes online only if you use it

  • AI requests go straight to Google Gemini: your level, the word count, an optional topic and the words you already have
  • A backup of your words, progress and settings goes to your own Google Drive, if you sign in with Google

A backup in your own Google Drive

Sign in with Google and ArtikelMeister keeps a backup of your words, progress and settings in your own Drive, in a folder called “ArtikelMeister Cloud Sync”.

Change something in the app and the backup updates on its own. Sign in with the same account on another device and your cards stay in sync. No “export” button to remember.

Bring your own word list

Got a vocabulary list from your course? Put it in a spreadsheet, save it as CSV and import it.

One row per word. Commas, semicolons or tabs all work. If a file looks wrong, the import stops before it touches your existing words.

Questions

Something else on your mind? Send an email or open an issue on GitHub.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no paid tier. The code is open source under the MIT license.

What language is the app in?

The app is in Turkish, built for Turkish speakers learning German, and meanings from the AI generator come in Turkish too. This website is in English and Türkçe.

Do I need a Google account?

No. Sign-in is optional: choose “Çevrimdışı Modda Devam Et” (continue offline) and you can study, import CSV files and keep your streak without one. Google sign-in is only needed for the Drive backup, and for the AI generator unless you use your own Gemini API key.

Does the AI generator cost money?

ArtikelMeister itself costs nothing. The generator runs on your Google account or your own Gemini API key, so Google's terms and limits apply. If you use your own key, any usage costs are yours.

How does the Drive backup work?

Once you sign in with Google, the app keeps a backup of your words, progress and settings in a folder called “ArtikelMeister Cloud Sync” in your own Drive. It updates automatically when you change something and keeps your devices in sync. The app can only access the files it created.

How do I delete my data?

In the app's settings, “Hesap Verilerini Sil” (delete account data) removes the Drive backup and the data on your device. You can also revoke the app's access in your Google Account permissions. The details are in the privacy policy.

Why isn't it on Google Play or the App Store?

Not yet. For now, every version is published on GitHub Releases.

Is there a Windows version?

It's planned. Android (8.0 or newer) and macOS (14 Sonoma or newer) come first.

Why is the icon a bleeding brain?

Because remembering articles hurts. Ask anyone who has spent ten minutes wondering why a girl, das Mädchen, is neuter.
